# Demo Recording Script (30-60s)

Use this script to record a short demo video or GIF for your Upwork portfolio.

1) Intro (3–5s)
- Show the project title and your headshot briefly.
- Overlay text: "TECHNEWS — Laravel 10 demo"

2) Homepage (10–12s)
- Show homepage, scroll a little to show featured articles.
- Narration: "Homepage with featured articles and search."

3) Article View (8–12s)
- Open an article, show content and images.
- Narration: "SEO-friendly slugs and rich content pages." 

4) Admin Dashboard (8–12s)
- Open dashboard, show stats and navigate to Articles.
- Narration: "Admin dashboard with article management." 

5) Create Article (6–8s)
- Show create-article form, fill title and brief content, click Publish.
- Narration: "Create and publish articles quickly."

6) CTA & Contact (3–5s)
- Show README Hire Me section or Upwork link.
- Narration: "Want this built? I'm available on Upwork — link in README."

Recording tips:
- Record at 1280×720 for landscape demos; crop to GIF if needed.
- Keep audio optional; add subtitles for silent autoplay.
- Use 30–60s total length.
